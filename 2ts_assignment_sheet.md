# PROJECT OVERVIEW AND TEAM TASK ASSIGNMENTS

Local farmers often face challenges in reaching customers directly, while consumers struggle to conveniently find fresh and affordable local produce. To solve this problem, our team is developing HarvestHub, a cross platform mobile application. This digital marketplace directly connects customers and farmers, allowing users to easily buy fresh goods while helping farmers showcase their products to a larger audience.
